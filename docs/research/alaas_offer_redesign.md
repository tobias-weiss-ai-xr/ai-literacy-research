# ALaaS Offer Redesign (transfer deliverable)

*Phase 6 transfer · 2026-08-23 · redesigns the AI-Literacy-as-a-Service offer
from `RESEARCH_PLAN.md` §5. Grounded in Guide 1 §4/§8, Guide 2 §5,
`assessment_spec.md` (tiers + SME variant), `compliance_mapping.md`.*

## Principle

Sell the **evidence chain**, not just training. Every tier delivers
*trained → measured → documented*, because that is the Art. 4-proof layer the
corpus says the market lacks (`compliance/evaluation` white space).

## Tiers

### Tier 1 — Readiness Mini-Audit (lead magnet → paid entry)
- **What:** 10-minute self-check (`readiness_mini_audit.md`); optional objective
  baseline on 4 tasks.
- **Evidence:** role × task × level snapshot.
- **Price:** free audit / €99 baseline add-on.
- **Upsell hook:** "You're in the white space — here's the fix."

### Tier 2 — Team Program (core)
- **What:** role-specific training (Guide 1 §4 playbook, compliance track) **+
  objective baseline/exit assessment** (AICOS or MAIL-CS/SFAILQ, population-
  matched; `assessment_spec.md` §3/§10).
- **Evidence:** per-role measured levels + stored session records (§8).
- **Delivery:** cohort, 4–6 weeks, task-based not quiz-based (Guide 2 §2.2).

### Tier 3 — Enterprise / Compliance
- **What:** Tier 2 + **Art. 4 evidence package** (`compliance_mapping.md` §4):
  role inventory, maturity diagnostic (AIconomics Maturity Model-style), aggregate
  readiness report, audit-ready documentation.
- **Evidence:** full chain role → task → level → stored record → report.

## Cross-sell / upsell logic
- Mini-audit → Team Program (show the gap).
- Team Program → Enterprise (show the compliance need + reporting layer).
- Reporting layer (Guide 2 §5) is the recurring, defensible differentiator.

## What changes vs a generic training offer
1. **Objective measurement is bundled, not optional** — counters the SR-vs-OB
   divergence (Guide 2 §2.2).
2. **Compliance track is first-class** (Guide 1 §8.1) — owns the `compliance/
   evaluation` white space.
3. **Audit-ready evidence** is the product, not a byproduct.
