## 1. The obligation (Art. 4, AI Act, in force 2 Aug 2024)

Providers and deployers must **ensure their staff and persons dealing with AI
systems have a level of AI literacy appropriate to their context**, considering
the technical understanding of AI and the context (data, domain, intended use).

Plain reading: *literacy is now a duty*, and "appropriate to context" means
**role-specific**, not generic. That is precisely the white space the corpus
flags.

## 2. Where the corpus says the gap is

`gap_analysis.md` ranks `compliance/evaluation` as the **#1 thin-and-surging
cell** (5 papers, +300% YoY). Translation: the field is starting to write about
Art. 4, but almost none of it *proves a compliance-readiness program worked*.
That is the opening — organizations need the "what did we actually do and can we
show it" layer.

The differentiator is the evaluation layer.

## 4. The mapping (obligation → action → evidence)

| Art. 4 element | What an organization must do | Corpus-grounded support |
|----------------|------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Identify who needs literacy | Role × AI-task inventory | `assessment_spec.md` §4 task matrices |
| Deliver appropriate training | Role-specific curricula | Guide 1 §4 playbook + §8.1 compliance track |
| Verify competence | **Objective**, task-based measurement | Guide 2 §2.1 (AICOS/MAIL-CS/SFAILQ) + §5.1 quasi-experiment |
| Document it | Stored evidence per role | `assessment_spec.md` §8 Art. 4 evidence chain |
| Show it on demand | Aggregate readiness report | Guide 2 §8.1 + gap cell targets |

## 5. Positioning: the "practical interpreter"

Most vendors sell *training*. The defensible position is the **evidence chain**
(row 4 → 5 above): train → measure objectively → store → report. That is the
artifact an auditor accepts, and it is exactly what the `compliance/evaluation`
gap shows the market lacks. The readiness mini-audit (`readiness_mini_audit.md`)
is the top-of-funnel version of this chain; the ALaaS baseline/exit instrument
(`assessment_spec.md`) is the full version.
