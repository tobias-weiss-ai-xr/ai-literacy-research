# AI-Literacy Readiness Mini-Audit (lead magnet)

*A 10-minute self-check for organizations wondering "are our people actually AI-literate?"
Built from the Assessment Instrument Spec (Phase 5) and the `compliance/evaluation`
white space (Phase 2 gap analysis). Not a scored assessment — a conversation
starter. The full version (task-based, objective, Art. 4-ready) is the ALaaS
baseline/exit instrument.*

## 1. Role coverage — who is in scope?

Tick the roles that *use or oversee* AI systems in your organization:

- [ ] Knowledge workers (office / ops)
- [ ] Customer-facing / support / SME staff
- [ ] Sales / revenue
- [ ] Developers / analysts / data
- [ ] Managers / team leads
- [ ] Compliance / risk / legal-adjacent

> **Gap signal:** if "Compliance / risk" is unticked but you deploy AI, you are
> exactly the `compliance/evaluation` white space the corpus flags as thin-and-
> surging — and where Article 4 exposure lives.

## 2. Task reality — what do they actually do with AI?

For each in-scope role, can you name **one real task** they perform with AI
(rather than "they use ChatGPT")?

| Role | Real AI task (workplace-specific) | Documented? |
|------|-----------------------------------|-------------|
|      |                                   |             |
|      |                                   |             |

> **Gap signal:** if you can't name a task, you have *tool adoption* without
> *literacy*. The instrument spec's occupation-aligned task matrices exist
> precisely to make this concrete.

## 3. Evidence level — can you prove it worked?

- [ ] We train people on AI
- [ ] We *measure* literacy with an objective instrument (not just a smile-sheet)
- [ ] We measure on the job's **actual tasks**, not quiz scores
- [ ] We keep a record per role (trained → measured → at what level)

> **Gap signal:** most organizations tick 1, few tick 2–4. That is the
> `org-implementation/evaluation` and `compliance/evaluation` white space —
> the field has instruments (MAIL-CS, AICOS, SAIL4ALL, SFAILQ) but almost no
> evidence programs *using* them worked.

## 4. Maturity read (one line per column)

| Level | You are here if… | Mark |
|-------|------------------|------|
| 1 Awareness | People know AI exists | ☐ |
| 2 Application | People use a tool on a defined task, check outputs | ☐ |
| 3 Evaluation | People pick tools by fit, judge outputs, handle edge cases | ☐ |
| 4 Integration | People design AI workflows, weigh consequences, teach others | ☐ |

## What your ticks say

- **Mostly Level 1–2, compliance unticked** → you are in the white space.
  Start with a compliance track + objective baseline (see Guide 1 §8.1, Guide 2 §8.1).
- **Level 3–4 in pockets, no measurement** → you have skill but no proof.
  Add the task-based evaluation design (Guide 2 §5) — that *is* your Art. 4 record.
- **All four evidence boxes ticked** → you are ahead of the corpus. Publish your
  method; you own the gap nobody else has filled.
